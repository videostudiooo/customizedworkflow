# Sound, captions and motion

## Audio

Use free local Agentcut capabilities by default.

- Voice drives timing when narration exists.
- Music sits under speech and supports the intended energy.
- Add SFX only at meaningful picture cues.
- Inspect audio with `agentcut_analyze_audio`, `agentcut_listen_measure` and `agentcut_listen_critique`.
- Do not claim a sound fits from listening intuition alone in the agent environment.
- A rough guide for online delivery is about -14 LUFS integrated with true peak at or below -1 dBTP; follow the user's platform or mastering requirements when supplied.

Do not silently select a paid provider.

## Captions

- Use source script wording when available; transcription supplies timing, not automatic spelling authority.
- Proofread names, numbers, products and URLs.
- Keep cues short, phrase-safe and non-overlapping.
- At 1080p, body text usually needs roughly 36 px or more; titles and phone-safe text are larger.
- Verify position against the delivered frame and platform UI safe zones.
- Deliver editable caption assets plus a burned-in version only when requested.

## Motion

- Motion must direct attention or express a change in meaning.
- Enter with ease-out, exit with ease-in, and use linear only for continuous progress.
- Keep staggers meaningful; do not animate every element with the same fade.
- Prefer a straight cut when there is no reason for a transition.
- Use Agentcut components before inventing a similar effect.
- Avoid repeated default gradients, floating blobs, random emoji and identical scene timing unless intentionally requested.
